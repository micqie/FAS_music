Parent Guide

📖 Parent Guide & School Policies

Please read these reminders before enrollment.

Please read these reminders before enrollment. Continuing means you acknowledge the school policies and terms.

🎵 Enrollment

  • Complete the enrollment form with accurate information.
  • A one-time Registration Fee of ₱1,000 is required.
  • Registration fees and payments are non-refundable.

💳 Payment Reminder

Remaining balances must be completed before the deadline below:

Package Payment Deadline
12 Sessions Before 7th Session
20 Sessions Before 11th Session
50 Sessions Before 30th Session

Failure to settle payments may temporarily suspend lessons.

📅 Attendance & Absences

12 Sessions → 2 allowable absences
20 Sessions → 3 allowable absences
50 Sessions → 5 allowable absences

If absent due to illness or emergencies, kindly request a Make-Up Session. Unused make-up lessons will still be counted as used.

❄ Schedule Freeze

Need to be away for 3 or more consecutive lessons? Request a Schedule Freeze to reserve your preferred schedule.

₱100 Slot Reservation Fee per missed session, credited toward future lesson payments.

⚠ Counted-In (CI) Policy

  • A lesson is considered used when the student is absent.
  • No prior notice was given.
  • The scheduled lesson time has already passed.

Please inform the school ahead of time to avoid a Counted-In lesson.

⏳ Lesson Validity

Unused lessons must be used within 3 months from the student's last attended lesson. After that period, remaining lessons may be forfeited unless approved by management.

🎒 Bring Every Lesson

  • Attendance Card
  • Music Book
  • Long Clearbook

🚫 Inside the Music Room

  • Food & drinks
  • Vapes or cigarettes
  • Hazardous materials
  • Unrelated electronic devices

📆 Scheduling

Follow your assigned schedule. Schedule changes depend on teacher and room availability. Please don't rely solely on reminder messages.

Missing a reminder does not exempt a student from the attendance policy.

🛡 Safety Reminder

Please keep your valuables secured. Father & Sons' Music is not responsible for lost or damaged personal belongings.

✅ Acknowledgement

By enrolling, parents and students acknowledge that they have read, understood, and agreed to the school's policies and terms.
